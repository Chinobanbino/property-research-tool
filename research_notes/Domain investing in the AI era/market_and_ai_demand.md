## 2. Scale and trajectory of the .ai boom

### Takeaway
.ai went from a niche ccTLD to more than 1M registrations (Jan 2026). Fees now make up nearly half of Anguilla's government revenue. NameBio-reported .ai aftermarket dollar volume roughly doubled in both 2024 and 2025 (about $22M in 2025). Registration momentum for "AI"-themed names cooled through 2026, and .ai aftermarket price growth has slowed.

### Cited Findings
- The .ai registry passed 1 million registrations in early January 2026 — [Sherwood News](https://www.sherwood.news/tech/now-more-one-million-ai-websites-contributing-an-estimated-70-million-anguilla-government-revenue/); [Gigazine, Jan 27 2026](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260127-anguilla-ai-domain-1-million)
- Anguilla .ai revenue:
  - 2023: about EC$87M (~US$32M) — [Sherwood News](https://www.sherwood.news/tech/now-more-one-million-ai-websites-contributing-an-estimated-70-million-anguilla-government-revenue/)
  - 2024: EC$104.25M
  - 2025: about EC$230–231M (~US$85M), more than double 2024. The 2025 jump was partly a renewal wave, because .ai has a 2-year minimum term — [Anguilla Focus](https://anguillafocus.com/ai-domain-surge-brings-ec230m-windfall-to-anguilla-in-2025/)
- Jan–Jul 2026: EC$217M (~US$80M), equal to 47.5% of Anguilla government revenue. Full-year 2026 government projection: EC$253M (~US$94M) — [Jamaica Gleaner, Oct 9 2026](https://jamaica-gleaner.com/index%2Ephp/article/business/20261009/anguillas-ai-domain-registration-earns-us80m-year-date-more-all-taxes). A cctld.ru projection of about $220M for 2026 conflicts with this and looks inconsistent (it may be in EC$) — [cctld.ru](https://cctld.ru/en/media/news/industry/39552)
- Identity Digital runs the .ai registry under a 5-year agreement signed in October 2024 — [Jamaica Gleaner](https://jamaica-gleaner.com/index%2Ephp/article/business/20261009/anguillas-ai-domain-registration-earns-us80m-year-date-more-all-taxes)
- The wholesale price rose from $70 to $80 per year on Mar 5, 2026. Vendor blogs report renewal rates of about 90%, and daily new registrations of about 2,008/day in Jan 2026 versus about 1,318/day in 2025. These are registrar/vendor-blog figures; treat them with caution — [BoldDomains](https://bolddomains.com/blog/ai-domain-statistics); [BoldDomains renewal](https://bolddomains.com/blog/ai-domain-cost-2026-renewal-and-the-two-year-rule)
- Aftermarket:
  - 2024: NameBio-recorded .ai dollar volume rose 107% — [Wikipedia: Domain aftermarket (citing NameBio)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain_aftermarket)
  - 2025: about $22M across 7,335 sales, with 27 six-figure sales — [TLD Investors](https://tldinvestors.com/2026/01/ai-2025-year-in-review.html)
  - 2025 .ai dollar volume was +90.6% vs 2024 — [NamePros/Bob Hawkes](https://www.namepros.com/blog/2025-domain-name-market-dollar-volume-trends-based-on-namebio-data.1374269/)
  - By Oct 2025, .ai had done $17.4M in 2025 sales, versus $18.3M across 2023 and 2024 combined — [TheDomains via search extract](https://www.thedomains.com/page/4)
- NameBio-based analysis puts .ai second among extensions by total transaction volume (undated extract). A vendor guide claims .ai is third globally by sales value after .com and .net — [NamePros threads](https://www.namepros.com/threads/1388685/); [DomainDetails guide](https://domaindetails.com/kb/domain-investing/ai-domain-investing-guide)
- InterNetX/Sedo Global Domain Report 2025: .ai aftermarket sales are booming while .io falls, and Sedo frames this as causal (substitution) — [DNJournal Lowdown Mar 18 2025](https://www.dnjournal.com/archive/lowdown/2025/dailyposts/0318.htm); [TheDomains](https://www.thedomains.com/2025/03/18/internetx-and-sedo-release-their-global-domain-report-2025)
- Cooling signal (vendor source): .ai aftermarket price growth is reported at only +7.7% YoY, which the source describes as "equilibrium" — [Dynadot trends / search extract](https://www.dynadot.com/blog/domain-name-trends)
- YC data: .ai overtook .io as the second most popular TLD among recent YC companies — [NameMesh](https://www.namemesh.com/guides/domain-naming/yc-startup-domain-name-trends)

### Inferences
- .ai registrations are still growing (revenue up again in 2026), but the aftermarket is shifting from fast price growth to volume. The premium tier (dictionary one-word .ai) remains strong, while speculative mid-tier .ai likely faces the $80/yr carry cost and 2-year minimum term.
- Concentration risk: the .ai boom depends on one small government and a policy-sensitive registry contract, a milder version of the .io sovereignty risk.

### Gaps
- No reliable median .ai sale price for 2025 or 2026 was found. NameBio publishes per-extension data, but the figures were not retrievable here.
- No authoritative figure for total .ai registrations as of Oct 2026 (only "1M+ in Jan 2026").

## 3. .io and the Chagos sovereignty transfer

### Takeaway
As of October 2026 the UK–Mauritius Chagos treaty (signed May 2025) has stalled and has not entered into force, mainly because the US withdrew support. Its implementing bill lapsed at the end of the 2024–26 parliamentary session (Apr 29, 2026). The .io registry is operating normally. The tail risk is long-dated: retirement would follow only if "IO" is removed from ISO 3166-1, with ICANN allowing at least 5 years for transition. .io is losing aftermarket share to .ai regardless.

### Cited Findings
- In April 2026 the UK told Parliament the implementing legislation could not complete passage in the 2024–26 session, which ended Apr 29. The treaty has not entered into force amid Trump's opposition — [Al Jazeera, Oct 2 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/2/uk-mauritius-chagos-sovereignty-deal-stalls-amid-fears-of-further-delays)
- Domain Incite: ".io safe for now as Trump puts Chagos deal on ice". The UK froze its plans for lack of US support; .io's future "seems dependent on Trump or his successor" — [Domain Incite](https://domainincite.com/31642-io-safe-for-now-as-trump-puts-chagos-deal-on-ice)
- ICANN (Nov 2024) said any transition would take at least 5 years after IO is removed from ISO 3166-1. The ISO list, not the treaty itself, is the trigger. The registry is operating normally and there is no retirement notice — [EuroDNS 2026 update](https://www.eurodns.com/blog/end-of-io-domain); [Wikipedia .io](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.io); [The Register, Oct 2024](https://www.theregister.com/2024/10/10/io_domain_uk_mauritius/)
- .io is run by a UK-based Identity Digital subsidiary and earns more than $40M/yr. Identity Digital also appears to have taken over the back-end for Mauritius's .mu, which may help it keep .io in any transition — [Domain Incite tag: BIOT](https://domainincite.com/tag/biot)
- Sedo data shows .io aftermarket sales falling as .ai rises — [TheDomains, Mar 2025](https://www.thedomains.com/2025/03/18/internetx-and-sedo-release-their-global-domain-report-2025)

### Inferences
- The near-term legal risk to .io has fallen in 2026, but the name now has a stigma: investors and startups price in sovereignty uncertainty and favour .ai or .com. That weakens .io aftermarket liquidity even if the TLD survives.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 .io registration counts or .io median sale prices were found.
- No news after Oct 2 2026 on the treaty's revival.

## 4. How AI changes naming demand (brandables vs exact-match, "AI" keywords, agentic names, cooling)

### Takeaway
AI is now the dominant buyer theme. "AI" was the top aftermarket keyword in 2025, and AI companies bought the biggest names (AI.com, Anything.com, Chat.com). Registration-level demand for generic "AI" names peaked around March 2026 and has stalled since. Vocabulary is moving to "agent/agentic" and speculative ".si" ("super intelligence"). Startups increasingly avoid "AI" in the brand itself and favour short brandables.

### Cited Findings
- AI was the most popular keyword in the domain aftermarket for most of 2025. In a small sample of the top 50 "*ai.com" sales, 44% had been put to use (developed or forwarded) — [Domain Name Wire, Sep 16 2025](https://domainnamewire.com/2025/09/16/the-market-for-ai-com-domain-names/)
- DNJournal (Feb 2025) headline: "The 2025 Domain Sales Season Starts the Way 2024 Ended - With .Com & .AI Large and In Charge" — [DNJournal](https://www.dnjournal.com/archive/domainsales/2025/0205.htm)
- Hostinger analysis (Oct 13 2026) of 6.2M registrations, Oct 2025–Sep 2026:
  - AI-related names grew only 1.5% from Apr to Sep 2026, versus 6.7% for all registrations. They peaked in March 2026 and the AI theme ranks 13th of 20.
  - "agent" names grew 14.6% and "agentic" names 16.7%.
  - Crypto terms fell (bitcoin −35.4%).
  - After a Sep 29 2026 US executive order on "super intelligence", Hostinger customers registered 34,582 .si names (Sep 23–Oct 7); only about 3% contain an AI term.
  - Caveat: Hostinger-only data, self-published — [Hostinger](https://www.hostinger.com/blog/domain-analysis-2026/); [ITNerd guest post, Oct 14 2026](https://itnerd.blog/2026/10/14/guest-post-from-ai-to-agentic-to-si-ai-domain-registrations-stall-as-naming-moves-on/); [Forbes India on .si](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/ai-tracker/why-slovenias-si-extension-is-suddenly-attracting-speculators/2999051/1)
- ICANN's new gTLD application list (Oct 7 2026) shows .agent tied for the most contested string, with 13 applicants — [Hostinger](https://www.hostinger.com/blog/domain-analysis-2026/); see also [NamePros: "Big Tech wants .Agent, investors are buying .SI"](https://www.namepros.com/blog/big-tech-wants-agent-investors-are-buying-si-but-where-are-the-end-users.1402646/)
- A .tech analysis of 6,420 startup domains found AI companies largely avoid putting "AI" in their names — [get.tech](https://get.tech/blog/how-tech-startups-are-naming-themselves/)
- InterNetX/Sedo Global Domain Report 2026 survey: 66% of respondents said AI affected demand, sourcing or sales in 2025 — [search extract; DNW on survey](https://domainnamewire.com/2025/11/20/internetx-and-sedo-open-survey-for-2026-global-domains-report/)
- Atom (June 2026): 124 NameBio-reported sales (opt-in only) totalling $550K. AI companies were among the buyers, e.g. HoplonAI.com at $8,500 — [Domain Name Wire, Jul 9 2026](https://domainnamewire.com/2026/07/09/atom-end-user/)

### Inferences
- Demand is splitting. (a) Ultra-premium one-word .com and one-word .ai bought by well-funded AI firms is strong and setting records. (b) Generic "X-AI" keyword registrations are saturated and cooling. (c) Speculation is rotating to new vocabulary (agent, agentic, SI) and new extensions, with unproven end-user demand.
- "Is the AI name bubble cooling?" Yes at the registration and speculative level (Hostinger data, .ai price growth ~+7.7%). No at the top end (AI.com, 1H-2026 volume +15% ex-AI.com).

### Gaps
- No marketplace-wide data quantifying the price premium of short .com versus brandables in 2026.
- No quantitative split of sales volume for "AI"-containing .com names in 2026.

## 5. AI search effects on type-in traffic, parking and exact-match SEO value

### Takeaway
The measurable 2025 shock to parking was not AI search but Google ending AdSense for Domains: parking revenue fell to "pennies on the dollar" and Sedo's Q3-2025 revenue fell 66%. AI answer engines are widely argued to erode exact-match-domain (EMD) SEO value and shift value to brand distinctiveness, but no hard data quantifies the effect on type-in traffic.

### Cited Findings
- In Feb 2025 Google said it would opt all advertisers out of showing ads on parked domains, and a final purge in Sep 2025 cut revenue to "pennies on the dollar". Team Internet Group laid off 200 staff; Sedo's revenue fell 66% in Q3 2025 — [Domain Name Wire, Dec 31 2025](https://domainnamewire.com/2025/12/31/2025-in-review-the-death-of-adsense-for-domains/)
- Google had already stopped auto-opting new advertisers into AdSense for Domains in Sep 2024 — [same](https://domainnamewire.com/2025/12/31/2025-in-review-the-death-of-adsense-for-domains/)
- One analysis found revenue per thousand sessions fell 51% while sessions fell only about 19%, so the loss was mostly monetization rather than traffic — [Bill Hartzer](https://www.billhartzer.com/?p=18461) (search extract)
- DNW (May 2025) warned that AI-changed search could reduce domain demand: Google was the backbone of discoverability for resale, lead-gen and type-in value, and fewer sites may be built — [Domain Name Wire, May 22 2025](https://domainnamewire.com/2025/05/22/ai-is-changing-search-and-that-could-impact-domain-demand/)
- Strategic Revenue: AI search may be "the biggest shift in the domain industry since the fall of the exact-match boom". It argues answer engines reward brand, trust and authority over keywords, eroding generic EMD SEO value — [Strategic Revenue](https://www.strategicrevenue.com/ai-search-may-be-biggest-shift-in-domain-industry-since-fall-of-exact-match-boom/)
- CircleID: much of domain investing still prices names "like it's 2015". As answer engines become referral channels, distinctiveness matters more than exact-match intent — [CircleID](https://circleid.com/posts/how-ai-answer-engines-are-quietly-rewriting-what-makes-a-domain-brandable)
- Measurement problem: many AI tools send no referrer, so AI-driven visits show up as "direct" traffic, which muddies type-in analysis — [Siteimprove](https://help.siteimprove.com/support/solutions/articles/80001188499-tracking-traffic-from-ai-tools-technical-considerations)

### Inferences
- Holding costs went up for portfolios that relied on parking income to cover renewals (AFD shutdown). That likely pushes investors to drop marginal names and price more aggressively for sales, adding supply to the aftermarket.
- EMD value is shifting from "SEO ranking asset" to "credibility/brand asset". Category-defining one-word .coms keep value; long-tail keyword EMDs lose it.

### Gaps
- No quantitative study of how AI Overviews or ChatGPT search affect domain type-in volumes or EMD sale prices was found; the sources are opinion or vendor content.

## 6. LLM name generators and competition for hand-registered brandables

### Takeaway
There is no hard data. Industry commentary says AI naming tools are now "standard equipment" for founders and namers. That likely increases the number of people able to find the same available brandables, while also helping end users name themselves without buying from investors.

### Cited Findings
- AI naming tools have "become standard equipment for professional namers and founders alike" in 2026 — [NameStation](https://www.namestation.com/blog/domain-naming-trends-2026)
- 5–7 character brandables are commanding premium attention, and auctions/drops require faster decisions and better research (commentary) — [DecideDomain](https://decidedomain.com/blog/domain-trends-2026); [NamePros thread](https://www.namepros.com/threads/top-5-domain-trends-you-should-watch-in-2026.1370233/page-2)
- GoDaddy began rebuilding its automated Domain Value Appraisal in Nov 2025 on an LLM-based stack, now live on Afternic — [GoDaddy blog](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/news/godaddys-domain-value-appraisal)

### Inferences
- LLM generators lower search costs on both sides. Founders can find available coined names themselves, which weakens demand for mid-tier investor brandables. Many investors chasing the same "LLM-obvious" coinages compresses hand-reg margins. These are reasoned inferences, not measured effects.

### Gaps
- No data on drop-catch competition intensity, hand-reg success rates, or adoption metrics for AI name generators.

## 7. Overall aftermarket volume, prices and liquidity

### Takeaway
Reported aftermarket volume is growing. NameBio recorded about $244M across about 190,300 sales in 2025 (+31.9% dollar volume vs 2024's about $185M across 144,700 sales). 1H-2026 recorded about $146M (+19.3% YoY). Growth comes mostly from more recorded sales, not higher prices. .com's share is slowly slipping toward ccTLDs, especially .ai. GoDaddy's aftermarket revenue is growing at mid-to-high single digits.

### Cited Findings
- NameBio 2024: 144,700 sales, US$185M — [Wikipedia: Domain aftermarket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domain_aftermarket)
- NameBio 2025: about 190,300 sales, just over $244M. Dollar volume was +31.9% vs 2024, attributed to more recorded sales rather than higher values. .com's share of dollar volume fell to 72.0% (from 74.4% in 2024); ccTLD share rose to 16.3% — [NamePros/Bob Hawkes](https://www.namepros.com/blog/2025-domain-name-market-dollar-volume-trends-based-on-namebio-data.1374269/). A different source, Corg, gives $314.7M for 2025, but its list has dating errors — [Corg](https://corg.com/biggest-domain-sales-2025-2026/)
- NameBio 1H-2026: about 128,300 sales, nearly $146M (+19.3% YoY). .com was about $107.4M (+17.6%) — [NamePros 1H-2026 analysis](https://www.namepros.com/blog/analysis-of-domain-name-sales-dollar-volume-reported-at-namebio-during-the-first-six-months-of-2026.1392654/)
- Coverage caveat: by some estimates NameBio holds only 5–10% of retail sales, and it skews to wholesale by count — [same NamePros 2025 analysis](https://www.namepros.com/blog/2025-domain-name-market-dollar-volume-trends-based-on-namebio-data.1374269/)
- NameBio trends page: .com average price about $788.79 over the trailing 30 days (21,871 sales; snapshot undated, average not median) — [NameBio Trends](https://namebio.com/trends)
- Sedo (InterNetX/Sedo Global Domain Report 2025, covering 2024 sales): average sale $2,345, median $549. 69% of sales were Buy Now, 11% Make Offer, 10% auction; 59% were .com; 350 different TLDs sold — [TheDomains, Mar 18 2025](https://www.thedomains.com/2025/03/18/internetx-and-sedo-release-their-global-domain-report-2025)
- GoDaddy aftermarket revenue:
  - Q1 2025: $128M (+5% YoY); average transaction value rose from $261 to $321 — [DNW May 2025](https://domainnamewire.com/2025/05/01/godaddy-reports-1-2b-revenue-in-q1-2025-domain-aftermarket-sales-rise-5/)
  - Q3 2025: $136M (+28% YoY; "large transactions returned") — [DNW Oct 30 2025](https://domainnamewire.com/2025/10/30/domain-aftermarket-revenue-surges-28-at-godaddy/)
  - Q2 2026: $129M (+9% YoY) — [DNW Jul 30 2026](https://domainnamewire.com/2026/07/30/godaddy-q2-2026/)
  - GoDaddy's own aftermarket portfolio holds about 1.0M names — [GoDaddy FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609711/000160971126000010/gddy-20251231.htm)
- Registration base (Verisign DNIB):
  - End-2025: 386.9M total (+6.2% YoY); .com 161.0M (+3.1%) — [Verisign IR](https://investor.verisign.com/news-releases/news-release-details/dnibcom-reports-internet-has-3869-million-domain-name)
  - End-Q2 2026: 401.6M total; .com 166.6M — [Verisign IR](https://investor.verisign.com/news-releases/news-release-details/dnibcom-reports-internet-has-4016-million-domain-name)

### Inferences
- The aftermarket is healthier in 2025–26 than registration growth alone suggests. Volume growth plus a rising average transaction value at GoDaddy points to steady end-user demand, with AI companies a major driver at the top end.
- .com is still about 72–74% of reported dollars, but the .ai share is the fastest-growing slice.

### Gaps
- No authoritative 2025/2026 median .com sale price (Sedo's $549 median is for 2024, Sedo only).
- No full-year 2025 GoDaddy aftermarket revenue total retrieved.
- No liquidity metric (sell-through rate) from a primary source was found for 2025–26.
