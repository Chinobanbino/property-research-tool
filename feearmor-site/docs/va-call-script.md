# FeeArmor VA Call Script (English + Español)
*Draft · Oct 21, 2026 · Follows the contracts research brief. Have a Texas attorney review before volume.*

## The 7 rules (never break these)

1. **Say who you are.** "I'm [your name] from FeeArmor, calling on behalf of [customer]." **Never say you are the customer. Never use the customer's name as your own.**
2. **Never ask for, hear or write down a PIN, password, security answer or one-time code.** The customer gives those straight to the provider. If one is said out loud, pause the recording and don't write it down.
3. **Announce the recording to everyone, on every call:** the customer first, then the provider rep. If anyone objects, stop recording and keep going.
4. **Every change needs the customer's YES.** Read the offer to them word for word: price, how long, contract, fees, equipment. No YES, no change.
5. **Never:**
   - change a SIM or number, PINs, passwords, contact details or authorized users
   - cancel a service or open a new line
   - make a payment
   - ask for call records
6. **Call hours:** 9am–9pm Monday–Saturday, noon–9pm Sunday, in the customer's time zone. No cold calls ever.
7. **Use only the FeeArmor system.** Never download bills, take screenshots, or copy account details into personal notes, chats or WhatsApp.

---

## Before the call (5 minutes)

- [ ] The customer's signed **Agreement** and **Authorization** are in the system, and the provider and account are listed.
- [ ] **Preferred language** noted (English/Español). Call in that language.
- [ ] The latest bill is open in the system. Note the starting monthly price, the fees to target, and the promo end date.
- [ ] Pick your 1–3 asks (see "What to ask for").
- [ ] Schedule the **3-way call** with the customer: "It takes about 20 minutes. You'll just need to verify yourself with [provider] and say yes or no to offers."

## ENGLISH SCRIPT

### 1. Call the customer first (recording starts)
> "Hi [customer], this is [your name] from FeeArmor. **This call is recorded** for quality and so we have a record of what's agreed. Is that okay?"
> "I'm going to add [provider] to the call. When they ask, please verify yourself directly with them. **Don't tell me any PIN or passcode.** Then I'll ask about your bill, and nothing changes unless you say yes."

### 2. Add the provider
> "Hi, **this call is being recorded.** My name is [your name], I'm with FeeArmor, and I'm on the line with your customer [customer full name], who has authorized us to help with their account. [Customer] is here to verify."

*(The customer verifies. If the rep asks for a PIN: "[Customer], please give that directly to them." Pause the recording if your system allows it.)*

> **Customer says:** "I authorize [your name] from FeeArmor to discuss my account and ask about lowering my bill."

### 3. Ask
Use the matching ask:

- **Expired promo or price jump:**
  > "Their bill went from $[old] to $[new] when the promotion ended. They'd like to stay, but not at this price. What offers can you apply to bring it back down?"
- **Equipment rental on owned equipment:**
  > "They're being charged $[x] a month for equipment rental, but they own their [modem/router]. Can you remove the rental charge and credit the months it was charged?"
- **Late fee on an on-time payment:**
  > "There's a $[x] late fee on [date], but the payment was made on [date]. Can you reverse it?"
- **Paper bill fee:**
  > "They'd like paperless billing and the paper bill fee removed. Can you credit it as well?"
- **Service they don't use:**
  > "They don't use [service]. What's the price without it?"

**Good follow-ups:**
- "Is there a loyalty or retention offer for long-time customers?"
- "Can I speak with the loyalty or retention team?"
- "What would a new customer pay for this same plan?"

### 4. Before saying yes, get every detail
Ask the rep:
- "What's the new monthly total, before taxes?"
- "How long does it last, and what's the price after?"
- "Is there a contract or early-termination fee?"
- "Any new fees, or changes to speed, channels, lines or equipment?"
- "When does it start, and which bill will show it?"

Then turn to the customer and read it word for word:
> "[Customer], the offer is: [change]. New price $[x] a month for [y] months, then $[z]. Contract: [none / details]. New fees: [none / details]. Equipment or service changes: [none / details]. **Do you want to accept? Please say yes or no.**"

- **YES:** "Thank you. [Provider], [customer] accepts."
- **NO:** "Thanks, we'll pass on this one."

**Never accept, even if the customer is unsure:**
- a contract or extension the customer didn't clearly approve
- slower speed or fewer channels they didn't clearly approve
- new equipment purchases they didn't clearly approve
- anything you can't explain clearly

### 5. Confirm and close
> "Can I get your name or ID and a confirmation number? And can you send [customer] a written confirmation?"

> "[Customer], we'll check your next bill to make sure it matches. You'll get a statement from us before anything is charged. Thanks!"

### 6. Log it right away (Savings Record)
Record each Fix in the Savings Record (date, provider, change, starting $/mo, new $/mo, savings/mo, months, confirmation #, rep name, "YES on recorded call [time]", bill to check). Then **set a reminder 45 days before the promo end date.**

### If the provider won't talk to you
- **"We can only speak to the account holder":**
  > "Understood. [Customer] is on the line and has verified. [Customer], could you tell them you authorize me to speak with them?"
- **They still refuse:** coach the customer to ask for the offer themselves, with you listening. Never pretend to be the customer.
- **The customer can't join:** reschedule. Don't call the provider without them unless the provider has the customer's written authorization on file.

## GUION EN ESPAÑOL

### Las 7 reglas (nunca se rompen)
1. **Diga quién es.** "Soy [su nombre] de FeeArmor, llamando de parte de [cliente]." **Nunca diga que usted es el cliente.**
2. **Nunca pida, escuche ni anote un PIN, contraseña, respuesta de seguridad o código de un solo uso.** El cliente se los da directamente a la compañía.
3. **Avise a todos que la llamada se graba**, en cada llamada. Si alguien se opone, deje de grabar.
4. **Cada cambio necesita el SÍ del cliente.** Léale la oferta palabra por palabra.
5. **Nunca:**
   - cambie la SIM o el número, los PIN, las contraseñas, los datos de contacto o los usuarios autorizados
   - cancele un servicio ni abra una línea nueva
   - haga pagos
   - pida el registro de llamadas
6. **Horario:** 9am–9pm de lunes a sábado, mediodía–9pm el domingo, en la zona horaria del cliente. Nunca llamadas en frío.
7. **Use solo el sistema de FeeArmor.** Nunca descargue facturas ni copie datos de cuentas en notas personales, chats o WhatsApp.

### 1. Llame primero al cliente (empieza la grabación)
> "Hola [cliente], habla [su nombre] de FeeArmor. **Esta llamada se graba** para control de calidad y para tener un registro de lo que se acuerde. ¿Está bien?"
> "Voy a agregar a [compañía] a la llamada. Cuando le pregunten, verifíquese directamente con ellos. **No me diga a mí ningún PIN ni código.** Después pregunto por su factura, y no se cambia nada si usted no dice que sí."

### 2. Agregue a la compañía
*(La mayoría de los representantes hablan inglés; pida la línea en español si existe.)*
> "Hola, **esta llamada se está grabando.** Me llamo [su nombre], soy de FeeArmor, y estoy en la línea con su cliente [nombre completo], que nos autorizó a ayudarle con su cuenta. [Cliente] está aquí para verificarse."

> **El cliente dice:** "Autorizo a [su nombre] de FeeArmor a hablar sobre mi cuenta y preguntar cómo bajar mi factura."

### 3. Pida
- **Se acabó la promoción:**
  > "Su factura subió de $[antes] a $[ahora] cuando terminó la promoción. Quiere quedarse, pero no a este precio. ¿Qué ofertas pueden aplicar para bajarla?"
- **Renta de módem propio:**
  > "Le cobran $[x] al mes por renta de equipo, pero el módem es suyo. ¿Pueden quitar ese cargo y acreditar los meses cobrados?"
- **Recargo por atraso en un pago a tiempo:**
  > "Hay un recargo de $[x] del [fecha], pero el pago se hizo el [fecha]. ¿Lo pueden reversar?"
- **Factura en papel:**
  > "Quiere factura electrónica y que le quiten el cargo por factura en papel."

### 4. Antes de aceptar, obtenga cada detalle
Pregunte:
- el nuevo total mensual
- cuánto dura y el precio después
- si hay contrato o penalidad por cancelar
- si hay cargos nuevos o cambios de velocidad, canales, líneas o equipo
- cuándo empieza

Luego léale al cliente:
> "[Cliente], la oferta es: [cambio]. Nuevo precio $[x] al mes por [y] meses, después $[z]. Contrato: [ninguno / detalles]. Cargos nuevos: [ninguno / detalles]. Cambios de equipo o servicio: [ninguno / detalles]. **¿Quiere aceptarla? Por favor diga sí o no.**"

### 5. Confirme y cierre
> "¿Me da su nombre o número de empleado y un número de confirmación? ¿Puede enviarle a [cliente] la confirmación por escrito?"

> "[Cliente], vamos a revisar su próxima factura para confirmar que coincida. Le enviaremos un estado de cuenta antes de cobrar cualquier cosa. ¡Gracias!"

### Si la compañía no quiere hablar con usted
> "Entiendo. [Cliente] está en la línea y ya se verificó. [Cliente], ¿les puede decir que me autoriza a hablar con ellos?"

Si aun así se niegan, guíe al cliente para que pida la oferta él mismo mientras usted escucha. **Nunca se haga pasar por el cliente.**

## Supervisor checks (weekly)
- Listen to 5 random recorded calls per VA. Check for:
  - the recording announced on both legs
  - the agent identifying as FeeArmor
  - no PINs heard or written
  - a YES before every change
- Check that every Savings Record row has a confirmation number and an approval time.
- Remove system access the same day a VA leaves.
