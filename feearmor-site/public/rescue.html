Quick answers

Do I need to have made any calls first?

No. Whether you made zero calls or ten, we start from your latest bill.

Do you need my passwords?

No. We start with a copy of your bill. If we need to contact a provider for you, we ask your permission first, and we never pretend to be you.

Can you guarantee savings?

No. Some bills are already clean, and providers decide whether to remove a fee. If we save you nothing, you pay nothing. If a provider doesn't honor a deal we got you, we fix it or refund that fee in full.

Is FeeArmor open?

Yes, in early access. Bill Rescue readers go to the front of the line, and we'll email you within one business day to start your free bill scan.
