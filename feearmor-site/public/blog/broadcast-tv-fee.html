What Is the "Broadcast TV Fee" on Your Cable Bill?

By Giovanni Morado · October 21, 2026

If you have cable or satellite TV, your bill may include lines like "Broadcast TV Fee" or "Regional Sports Fee." These aren't government taxes. They're charges the TV company adds to cover what it pays to carry local stations and sports networks.

What changed: "all-in" pricing

In 2024 the FCC adopted rules requiring cable and satellite TV companies to show an "all-in" price, including these kinds of fees, clearly in their ads and on bills, instead of hiding them in the fine print. The rule changes how the price is shown. It doesn't ban the fees or cap them.

What you can do

  • Know your real total. Add these fees to your package price when you compare plans.
  • Ask about plans without sports networks if you don't watch them. Some fees depend on the channels in your package.
  • Ask for a credit or a better offer if the fee went up and pushed your bill higher than you expected.
  • Compare streaming or antenna options for local channels if you mostly watch local TV.
Say this: "My Broadcast TV fee went up and my total is higher than I expected. What package or offer can you give me to bring my total back down?"

Rules and fees can change. Check your provider's current terms and the FCC's website for the latest.
