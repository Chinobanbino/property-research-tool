FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you offer service in Spanish?

Yes. Our team speaks Spanish. From reviewing your bill to calling your providers, everything can be in Spanish, and your agreement and authorization come in Spanish too. Ver en español.

Is FeeArmor open?

Yes. We're open in early access and take new customers in the order they sign up. We'll email you within one business day to start your free bill scan.

Which bills do you work with?

Internet, mobile phone, cable and satellite TV, and fees on electric and gas bills. If a bill comes every month, there's a good chance we can check it.

How much does it cost?

Joining the list is free. When we save you money, you pay a one-time fee of 40% of your first year of savings, billed only after the lower price shows up on your bill. You can split it into 3 monthly payments. Everything after the first year is 100% yours. Save nothing, pay nothing.

Is there a contract or long-term commitment?

No long-term commitment. You sign a short service agreement, approve each change before we accept it, and can cancel anytime. You only pay a one-time fee on savings that show up on your bill.

Can you guarantee I'll save money?

No. Some bills are already clean, and the provider decides whether to remove a fee. What we promise instead: if we save you nothing, you pay nothing. And if a provider doesn't honor a deal we got you, we fix it or refund that fee in full.

Do you need my account passwords?

No. We start with a copy of your bill. If we need to contact a provider for you, we'll ask your permission first, and we never pretend to be you.

Can you lower my electric or gas bill?

Some utility charges are set by regulators and can't be negotiated, and we don't switch your electricity plan. What we can do is catch fees charged in error, late fees on payments made on time, and billing mistakes.

What do you do with my information?

We use your bills only to find and fight fees for you. We never sell your data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
